Services

Two Ways We Bring
A Royal Into Your Life

Whether you're just starting your search or your puppy is waiting on the other side of the world, here's exactly how each part of the process works.

Apply For A Puppy

Finding Your Match

We keep our litters small and our families close, so every application gets a real, personal look — never a first-come-first-served scramble.

1

Tell Us About You

Complete our short application with your household, lifestyle, and what you're hoping for in a companion — coat, size, temperament, and timing all matter.

2

We Match You

We review applications personally and reach out with puppies who genuinely fit your home, rather than simply the next name on a list.

3

Welcome Home

Once you've said yes, we handle health documentation and prep on our end, and coordinate pickup or delivery — including export, if you're not local.

Start Your Application

Export Services

Bringing Your Royal Home, Wherever You Are

We work with approved families around the world, arranging safe, documented, and well-prepared international travel for every puppy we export.

1

Preparation

Every puppy is vet-checked, vaccinated on schedule, microchipped, and given time to reach a safe, healthy travel weight before any export is arranged.

2

Documentation

We prepare the health certificates, vaccination records, and import paperwork required by your destination country, working directly with our veterinarian to keep everything accurate and current.

3

Travel Arrangements

Depending on the destination, we coordinate an approved pet courier or in-cabin flight nanny, always prioritizing the shortest, most comfortable route for the puppy.

4

Health & Safety Standards

We only fly puppies when temperatures, airline policies, and the puppy's own readiness align. We would rather delay a flight than rush one.

5

Arrival & Support

We stay in contact through every leg of the journey and remain available to your family long after your puppy arrives home.

Have a destination in mind? Reach out and we'll walk you through what's involved.
