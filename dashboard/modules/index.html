Our Story

Dollhaus Royale

A simple love for Shih Tzu

Dollhaus Royale began with a simple love for Shih Tzu. Our journey started in 2017 with Luna, and years later led us to Ottie, Cheesecake, and eventually a mentor who opened the door to a deeper understanding of Shih Tzu genetics and bloodlines.

That journey became Dollhaus Royale — a home where every puppy represents everything we have learned along the way.
